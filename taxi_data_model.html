Reference tables (not in the notebook output)

  • taxi_zone: TLC zone lookup (LocationID, zone, borough, geometry). Needed to label zones and to join traffic links to zones.
  • payment_type: TLC code list (0 flex fare, 1 credit card, 2 cash, 3 no charge, 4 dispute, 5 unknown, 6 voided). Tip features are only meaningful for card trips.

Caveats

  • good_tip counts cash trips (tip = 0) as bad tips, which pulls down zone percentages. Filter to payment_type == 1 for a fair propensity.
  • tip_percent is Inf/NaN when fare_amount is 0; good_tip treats these as 0.
  • Small-volume zones give extreme percentages (e.g. zone 199 at 83%, others at 0%); consider a minimum trip count.
  • Traffic and taxi dates are selected independently, so a join on time needs overlapping dates.
