How these numbers are worked out
Data
Cleaned TLC yellow-taxi trips with pickups Aug 1 – Sep 30, 2026 (the same 6,767,274 rows the notebook loaded), joined to the TLC taxi zone lookup.
Trip duration
Drop-off time minus pickup time, in minutes.
Profitability index
(fare + tip − tolls) ÷ trip duration in minutes.
Tipping propensity
Card trips with tip ÷ fare > 0.20, as a share of all card trips in the zone. Zones with very few card trips can show 100% or 0%; use the minimum-trips filter to hide them.
Avg tip %
Mean of tip ÷ fare. Trips with a $0 fare are left out of this average.
Pickup activity
The notebook's pickup_activity() for Aug 1 → Aug 2: trips that picked up on or after Aug 1 and dropped off by midnight Aug 2, divided by 1 day.
OD probability
100 × trips from A to B ÷ all trips from A. Trips to or from zones without a name (the TLC "Unknown" / "Outside of NYC" codes) are left out, as pandas drops them when grouping.
