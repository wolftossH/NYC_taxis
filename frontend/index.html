    How the numbers work. Pickups per day = pickups ÷ selected days. Earning rate (profitability index) = (base fares + recorded tips − tolls) ÷ occupied minutes, so it excludes operating costs and empty driving. Card tipping = card trips with a tip over 20% of the fare ÷ card trips (cash tips are not recorded), by pickup zone or, for the dropoff version, by the zone and time where the trip ends. Avg card tip = card tips ÷ card fares. Dropoffs per day = trips ending in the zone during the selected time of day ÷ selected days; pickups per dropoff = pickups ÷ dropoffs, so above 1 means more fares start in the zone than end there. Destination % (origin–destination probability) = trips from the zone to a destination ÷ all selected trips leaving the zone; the top 10 are listed. All rates are recomputed from summed totals for the selected period.

    Limits. This is history, not a live forecast. Zones with few trips are greyed out; short time windows (15 minutes) hold few trips, so raise the minimum or widen the window when a result looks noisy. Traffic speeds (NYC DOT) start on 2026-08-01, with no data for 2026-10-12. The indexed chart compares earning rate and traffic on the selected days that have both; when none do, it compares daily shapes from different days and says so. Traffic is a borough roll-up of road links, not mapped to taxi zones.
