    How the numbers work. Pickups per day = pickups ÷ selected days. Fare+tip rate = (base fares + recorded tips) ÷ occupied minutes, so it excludes operating costs and empty driving. Card tipping = card trips with a tip over 20% of the fare ÷ card trips (cash tips are not recorded). Destination % = trips from the zone to a destination ÷ all selected trips leaving the zone. All rates are recomputed from summed totals for the selected period.

    Limits. This is history, not a live forecast. Zones with few trips are greyed out. Traffic covers one day and is chosen independently of the taxi dates.
